Вопросы · HELPFUL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our fresh milk ice creams, storage tips, wholesale orders, and distribution in Uzbekistan.

Ingredients & Quality

What kind of milk does Dairy Classic use in its ice cream?

We use 100% real, fresh liquid milk and pure dairy cream collected daily from regional farms in Uzbekistan. We never use reconstituted milk powders, artificial milk solids, or hydrogenated palm oils.

Are Dairy Classic ice creams Halal certified?

Yes. All Dairy Classic ingredients, processing facilities, and manufacturing methods follow strict Halal food guidelines. Our chocolate contains pure cocoa butter and our vanilla is alcohol-free.

Where is Dairy Classic ice cream made?

All 44 of our ice cream flavours are made in our modern, certified facility in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, operating continuously since 1996 under strict HACCP and ISO food quality standards.

Storage & Shelf Life

What is the recommended storage temperature?

For optimal taste, creamy texture, and crisp cone crunch, our ice cream should be stored at -18°C (0°F) or colder. Avoid keeping ice cream in refrigerator doors where temperature fluctuates.

What is the shelf life of Dairy Classic products?

Our individually wrapped cones, bars, sandwiches, and family tubs maintain peak freshness for 12 months when continuously kept at -18°C.

Why are Dairy Classic waffle cones so crispy?

We bake our waffle cones fresh in-house and spray the inside with a delicate micro-layer of pure chocolate before filling. This moisture barrier keeps the waffle crisp until the very last bite.

Wholesale & Business Supply

How can my grocery store or supermarket sell Dairy Classic?

Contact our wholesale team via phone or WhatsApp. We provide competitive trade pricing, scheduled refrigerated delivery, and can install branded display freezers for qualified retailers.

What is the minimum wholesale order quantity?

Minimum order sizes depend on your location. Within Tashkent City, minimum orders start from just 3 cartons. For Samarkand and regional shipments, our sales team will recommend a starter bundle.

Do you supply cafes, restaurants, and catering events?

Yes. We offer 1-litre family tubs (Belgian Chocolate, Pistachio Delight, French Vanilla, Mango) and multi-layer Cassatta cakes that are ideal for dessert menus and celebratory banquets.

Still have questions?

Our team in Tashkent is ready to assist you Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
